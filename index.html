 a note from the workbench

A little imperfect.
Very much on purpose.

This collection starts with familiar UI patterns and asks a small question: what if they felt more human?

Buttons become stamps. Tabs become binder dividers. Progress becomes a quilt. A first idea becomes a seed.

There are 100 distinct, working experiments across 10 chapters. Every one has its own HTML page. Everything works locally, without a build server or external services.

How to explore

Search a feeling, a pattern, or a task. Open a component to play with it. Try the phone view. Keep your favorites in a local shortlist. Peek at the HTML when something sparks an idea.

Take a piece. Make it your own. Keep the heart.
